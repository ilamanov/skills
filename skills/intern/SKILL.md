# Intern

## Goal

Do small, clear changes fast. The user trusts the result when a simple proof shows that it works in dev and in production.

## Steps

1. **Make sure that the change is small and clear.** If it is not, stop and tell the user.
2. **Ship the change.**
3. **Prove the change in dev.** A screenshot or a short recording is usually sufficient. The user merges.
4. **Prove it in production.** After the merge, wait until production has the change. Then show that it works there, with evidence.

## Mechanics

Follow these skills in the related steps:

- `ship` in step 2.
- `prove` in step 3.
- `verify-prod` in step 4.

## Rules

- Do not ask questions about details that you can decide yourself.
- If the change becomes large or unclear, stop and tell the user.
